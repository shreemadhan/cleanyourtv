# TCL 75T89C (75", model "Smart TV Pro", device G08, G08_4K_GB, MediaTek tcl9618), India
# Android 14 (Google TV), firmware UTT2.250416.001 AU02, security patch 2025-12-05. About 2.4 GB RAM.
# Same G08 board as examples/tcl-g08-us-google-tv and examples/tcl-g08-eu-android12, on the India build
# (no US streaming preloads; ships TCL Channel, TCL Live free channels and a TCL account service).
#
# Tested 30 October 2026 on one set, about one day of use. Disabled by hand with `pm disable-user --user 0`,
# one call each, in batches with a screenshot between them. The first 26 packages were reboot-checked
# (pm list packages -d compared before and after, identical). The last three content apps, the stock
# launcher and the setup wizard went in around the reboot and have been through one. Not weeks of use.
#
# Apply:   TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/disable.sh examples/tcl-75t89c-g08-android14/debloat.txt
# Undo:    TV_HOST=<tv ip> tools/restore.sh examples/tcl-75t89c-g08-android14/debloat.txt com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx
# Everything is DISABLED, not uninstalled. Comment out any line you want to keep.
#
